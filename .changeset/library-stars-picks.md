Find and come back to designs. Home gains a Library view: every mock with all its variants, each pictured whole with its status, version, lineage and a star, filtered by pages or components, starred, or status. Star any variant from its stage or the Library to mark a favourite (it decides nothing; agents see `starred` in `read`). Picks groups the chosen designs by mock with how many states are decided, and shows the starred ones on a switch. The canvas opens at a readable size on the first mocks, with a minimap of the rest; Fit all shows everything. The guide now tells agents to build screens from components.
