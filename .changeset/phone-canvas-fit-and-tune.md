On a phone or iPad, the canvas now opens a design as one whole screen of its device (a 390 design shows its full 390×844 screen), and shrinks it to fit above or below the question card while the card is open. A pinch on the design itself zooms the canvas, down to half of that fit; tap the scale for true size. Tuned knob values now survive a theme switch instead of falling back to the defaults on the live frame. The color picker opens at full height on iOS Safari, and the header's menu opens above the question card instead of under it.
