Mockpit is now two places: the Board to see a project, Focus to decide on one design. Every link, the agent's included, opens on the Board fit to that design or state, selected; Enter, a double-click or Open ↵ opens Focus, and Esc goes back up. Home's shelves and area page, the area chips, Studio (Answers, Wall, Columns, Palettes), All states and its grid, the Stage | Studio switch, the view switch, the question counts in the project menu and the glow on covers are gone, and a design no longer opens differently by what it holds; old `?view=` and `?cols=` links land on the Board.

The Board lays a project out as a designer's file is: a page per area, a section per flow with its steps across and the screen's other states stacked under the step they follow, then each screen's states in no flow, the components the page uses, each variants design as a grid (variants down, states across) and what is planned, the sections wrapping in rows so a page fits on screen. Every frame is its still under its name, never a running page; the states a screen is still owed are drawn dashed as missing, and a grid shows its first rows with "N more ▾" for the rest. Pan by dragging empty space, with Space held or by scrolling; zoom with ⌘-scroll, a pinch or + and -; Shift 1 fits the page, Shift 2 the selection, and ⌘/ hides the rail and the cards. A selected frame offers Copy link, an address that opens fit to it. ▶ Present (or P) plays a flow full screen, → and ← walk it, R starts over and Esc ends it on the Board. The rail lists Needs you, the one inbox, which folds out every open question and opens Focus on it; the pages, each with its Ready designs ("3/7") and the open page's sections and frames under it; the Library (Components, System, Documents); and Exploring (Ideas, Archive, Recent). `GET /api/projects/:name/board` reads one page.

Each page's card says what's left on it: a coverage map of the area's screens and components down and every state across, each cell Ready, In design, Planned or Missing, with each flow's "N of M steps ready". A cell fits the Board to its frame, a flow's line to its section, and Hide map folds the card to its one line. `mockpit read --area` carries the same map as `coverage`, as text an agent reads.

Ideas are on the Board. The Ideas page lays out every idea as a dashed section, its alternatives down and its states across, labelled "idea for <design>" or "becomes <kind>"; a lab's states are each a column of their own alternatives. "Ideas · N" on a screen's section unfolds its ideas right under it, dashed and dimmed. Archived alternatives fold under "N archived", and a section with more than 20 alternatives has a filter on its header. The Board shows what the agent did: "agent…" on the frame it is writing to until the new still lands, its section outlined for a moment, a dot on each frame changed since this browser last had it on screen, and "Since you were here: 3 changed" on the card, which fits to each in turn. A question waits on its frame as a "?".

Focus is the design live, its states on the left (not drawn ones dashed, then its variants, or an idea's alternatives) and Question, Spec (with Instances) and Activity on the right; its path reads project / page / design. A question with pictures has One | Side by side: Side by side lays the design as it stands and every answer at full height in a row, a click picks one, outlined and named Chosen while the rest dim, and after a Send the row stays with the pick outlined, kept answers beside it and archived ones folded. Compare with… shows another version 2-up in one frame with a divider to drag, and an alternative changed since you saw it offers Compare with what you saw. The note on a product design says what it is ("New version · #10", "Changed since Ready") before its Back to #9. On an idea the panel offers More like this, Borrow, Keep, Move and Archive, and C takes up Comment. The palette to view frames in is picked beside Light and Dark.

On a phone the Board is the page's sections as rows you swipe sideways, and Focus is one frame you swipe through the states of, with Question and Spec as sheets over a bar; the question comes up by itself when one waits, a tap on an answer picks it and shows it on the frame, and Send sits in the sheet. From 700 to 1099 px the canvas takes touch (one finger pans, two pinch, a double-tap opens Focus) and the rail slides in from ☰. The agent's guide, the CLI help, the MCP descriptions and the README speak of the Board and Focus, the link `edit --project --area` returns opens that area's page, and `mockpit demo` takes the stills the Board shows.
