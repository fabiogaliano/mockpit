On a phone, the mock screen has one scroll and one zoom. The design is a single page fitted to the width that the browser scrolls natively, sideways too once zoomed. A pinch zooms only the design and never scales the viewer around it. The questions are a sheet fixed to the bottom instead of a card that can be dragged and docked. A tap or a swipe folds the sheet to one line naming the current question, the question is its headline, and word answers stack as full-width rows.
