Using another alternative of an idea onto its screen or component replaces the entry the idea's last use landed on, instead of adding one more. On a variants design, or a state holding several entries, a first use whose name no entry has adds an entry, and the reply says so: `usedIn.copies[].added` and a plain `usedIn.note`; the viewer's toast says it was added beside what the design had.
