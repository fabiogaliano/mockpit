"mockpit": minor

Mix can no longer contradict a choice you already made. It skips parts that an answered question or a tuned part knob decided, and it closes wherever you answered a question in words. A borrow that a later answer contradicts is dropped from the draft. Mix never offers a part that fills the whole frame. A borrow previews on the stage with the part spotlit: hover it, or tap it on touch. Mix is marked "Optional · from mockpit". The "Mix uses … instead · undo" override is gone.

Nudges: a new variant is bound to your existing Look ask when every state has the same variants, and gets a state ask otherwise. `ask` flags visual-sounding options with no `variant` or `set`, and plain asks placed next to a Look ask. The brief and skills now say: a variant decides everything it differs on, and visual options carry a `variant` or knob `set`.
