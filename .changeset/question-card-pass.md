The floating question card, revised. Word answers are full-width rows that wrap, wherever the card rests; the question is the headline and its topic a small label. Accept / Revise / Drop and the note fit a narrow card instead of spilling past its edge. Dragging moves the card itself, lifted under the finger, and it glides into the spot it lands on. A fold button shrinks it to one line (the question it is on), which a tap opens again.
