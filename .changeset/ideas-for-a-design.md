Ideas know what they are for. An idea names the screen or component it explores (`publish --for onni`, `edit onni-lab --for onni`, `"for"` on MCP and HTTP), or, for nothing yet, what it would become (`--becomes screen`, `component`, `flow` or `system`); a publish nudges an idea that says neither and looks like it is for a design. On such an idea the button reads "Use in Onni": the version you use is copied onto Onni as the next version of the state of the same name, current at once with the way back a newer version has, and you stay in the idea, which reads "In use in Onni". When the idea draws it in a state Onni lacks, nothing is copied and the agent hears `followUps.apply` with the states that matched and those missing. An idea that would become a screen or component makes it on first use and is then for it. The agent exploring for a design reads what that design already turned down (`target.ruledOut`: archived alternatives, archived answers and its decisions' "Not:") on every read of the idea and in each reply on it. A screen or component lists its ideas folded under the states and in Studio's bar ("Ideas · 2"), its cover on Home carries a quiet count that opens the Ideas shelf showing only those, and the Ideas shelf groups by Area, For or In use, remembered per project. New idea is typed by where you start it: a shelf gives what it would become, the area in view its area, and a design's ⋯ "New idea for this" what it is for. `read --tree` carries `for`, `becomes` and `ideasFor`, and `read --area` groups the ideas waiting by what they are for.
