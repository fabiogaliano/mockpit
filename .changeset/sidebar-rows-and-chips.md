The Home sidebar no longer mixes mockpit's words with a project's. Ideas, Screens, Flows and Components are rows with icons, and a project's areas are chips below them: press one to filter, press it again to take the filter off. Only Needs you has a number, with no dots beside it. The question count at the top right is gone where the sidebar is showing, and "Answer next" now sits on the Needs you page. The count for all your projects moves into the project menu, except on a phone or where there's no sidebar.
