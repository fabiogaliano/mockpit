Viewer fixes from a first look at the user-driven review. Try, Comment, Tune, the preview (widths, the QR) and the palette with light or dark are back in their tool strip against the design's left edge, out of the top bar and the ⋯ menu; a phone keeps what it had. A cell opened from the Sheet steps to the next frame or value from the stage (Shift and the arrows, or the control above the frame that says where it is), keeps P, M and X on the cell, and Esc or Sheet goes back to the sheet on the cell left. Focus's shortcuts, the Sheet's among them, are behind `?`, as the Board's are. The mark pill is sized by the frame or cell it sits on, and a sheet cell spans its column up to its still's width. The question panel's head lines up on shared edges, with List, Pictures and Side by side as one control under the question. The side panel's toggle has a side-panel icon. A part's name tag stays inside the frame when the part touches its top or bottom edge.
