Mockpit speaks the industry's words. What you choose between is an **alternative**: `publish --alternative <name>`, `alternatives` in reads and the tree, question options bound with `alternative`, and the built-in question reads "Which alternative?" (MCP and HTTP the same; `--variant` on an idea now fails, naming `--alternative`). A **variant** is a form that ships: `publish --variants` or `edit <slug> --variants` says a screen's or component's entries all ship (a component's sizes, tones or feelings), so they are named with `--variant`, `read` lists them as `variants`, and nothing on it is chosen or nudged into an idea; `--no-variants` turns it off. Stored replies, drafts, questions, kept answers, forks and copies are rewritten in place on the first boot. What can go in a part is a **swap**: `data-swap` (with `data-swap-label`, `data-swap-default`), `publish --swaps part#swap=file` (MCP and HTTP `swaps`), an answer's `swap` beside its `set`, `suggestedSwapAsks`, and export's `allSwaps` and `all-swaps.html`; stored pages say `data-swap` on every part that had `data-fill`. An included component is an **instance**: `<mockpit-instance slug="card">` (its wrapper says `data-mockpit-instance`), `instances` and `instanceIn` in reads and the tree, and the design screen's Built from panel is titled Instances; stored pages, current and past, are rewritten to the new tag. A named combination of answers is a **combo** (`combos` on `read`, a reply's `combo`, Combos beside the design), and the project's decision list is its **decision log** (`decisionLog` on every read). A design opens by what it is, and nothing is remembered per design: a question the agent is waiting on opens Answers, an idea opens in Studio, a screen or component on Stage with **All states** as its second view, and a variants design as its grid. Each level has one status ladder: a screen or component reads In design, Ready or Changed; an alternative Kept, In use or Archived; a version current or Hidden. Taking an answer out of a question reads Archive, and Studio's bookmark reads **Keep**, with **Move** beside it.
