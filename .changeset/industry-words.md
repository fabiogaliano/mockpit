Mockpit speaks the industry's words. What you choose between is an **alternative**: `publish --alternative <name>`, `alternatives` in reads and the tree, question options bound with `alternative`, and the built-in question reads "Which alternative?" (MCP and HTTP the same; `--variant` on an idea now fails, naming `--alternative`). A **variant** is a form that ships: `publish --variants` or `edit <slug> --variants` says a screen's or component's entries all ship (a component's sizes, tones or feelings), so they are named with `--variant`, `read` lists them as `variants`, and nothing on it is chosen or nudged into an idea; `--no-variants` turns it off. Stored replies, drafts, questions, kept answers, forks and copies are rewritten in place on the first boot.
