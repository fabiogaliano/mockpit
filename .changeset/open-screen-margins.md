Opening a design is now the design with what's about it at its sides. States and versions stand on the left, the question and your comments on the right, with pins on the design; each side appears only with something in it, so a plain render is the design alone. Try, Comment and Tune sit in a small strip left of the design, and only those that apply show. On this design, Built from and Thread open from icons in the top bar, beside Revise and Use this. A question's options are pictures you can read, previewed on hover. Everything a Send will carry is listed beside it: comments can be reworded or removed before they go, and comments or tuning can go without an answer. A wide page keeps its size: when a margin would take the design below 80% it folds to its tab and opens floating over the canvas, so a 1280 page shows at 87% at 1280 wide and 80% with the question standing beside it at 1440. A width you pick is now the only one on the canvas, with All widths to spread them, and a design with one width has no menu. Studio and the phone are as they were, except "Only ones I used" is gone from the phone's History and Revise no longer competes with Send in white.
