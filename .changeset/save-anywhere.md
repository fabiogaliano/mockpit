The bookmark on a Studio card opens one menu for where a version lives: tick any shortlists, mocks or states, flip "Remove from here" to move it rather than copy it, and the button says what will happen, with Undo after. Saving works from a shortlist too. A saved `default` takes its origin's name, so it can't become what a component shows wherever it is embedded.
