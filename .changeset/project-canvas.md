The project as a tree. On a wide screen Home is a canvas beside the mock list: every mock is a cluster with its chosen design large, the open alternatives beside it and archived ones folded into a stack that fans out, pages above the components they are built from, joined by their embeds. Pan by dragging or scrolling, zoom with a pinch or the buttons; a row's tree button finds its mock on the canvas. On a phone the same button opens the tree one level at a time: a mock's variants, the components it is built from and where it is used. Agents read the same tree with `read --tree` (MCP `read({tree: true})`).
