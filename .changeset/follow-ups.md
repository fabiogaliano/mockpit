A decision now says what it requires. Each feedback batch carries `followUps`, worked out by mockpit from the reply and what it changed, each by what and where: `apply` (an alternative used in a design whose states it couldn't drop into, to publish there), `gaps` (the states, or on a variants design the variants, the design has that the used alternative doesn't draw), `instanceIn` (the screens and components that show a component that just changed, each with whether it was Ready), `spec` (product designs whose decisions are newer than their spec, with the count) and `lift` (a screen just marked Ready, as before). `apply` and `lift` move there from the reply and the batch. The guide tells the agent to update a spec, apply on the target and fill gaps itself, and to bring anything that touches another design back as an idea for it with one question, so you choose. `read` without a design lists each design's follow-ups still open under `pending`, so an agent that restarts finds them, and the CLI's `watch` and `read`, MCP and the pi extension print them. Nothing new to look at in the viewer: what the agent does shows in the Thread, and what needs you arrives as a question.
