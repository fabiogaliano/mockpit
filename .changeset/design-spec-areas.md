A design carries its own record, so decisions stop living in side files: notes become the **spec**, under What it is for, How it works and Decisions (the design's lines of the decision log). Agents write it with `edit --purpose` and `edit --behaviour "…" --state Now --part river`, and a line pinned to a part shows as a numbered pin on the stage while the Spec panel is open. A section you write by hand stands against agents until you clear it, and the agent hears your edit as `edited.spec`; a screen or component with no spec, or with decisions newer than it, is nudged on publish and in its feedback. Areas say what they are for (`edit --project <p> --area Food --description "…"`, or in place on the area page), and so does the project; `read --area Food` (MCP `read` with `area`, `GET /api/projects/:name/areas/:area`) gives an agent everything settled in one area, and Home's area page is the same view: description, screens, flows, components, decisions and the ideas waiting. Home's sidebar is now Product (Screens, Flows, Components, System), then the areas, then Ideas, and opens on the product. `edit --area-order` is now `edit --areas`, on every tier including the pi extension; existing notes and area orders move over in place.
