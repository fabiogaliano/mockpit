The Sheet: a grid of every frame of a state down and the values of one choice knob (a select or image knob), or the design's looks, across. Up to 12 columns. A One / Sheet switch after the design's name shows it wherever there are two or more frames and a choice knob or a look. In Tune, Spread beside a choice knob lays out the frame on stage in every value, and All widens that to every frame. On a screen or a component, the sheet opens in an idea for it that holds copies of its frames and is reused the next time, so the product design is never changed. A cell is cropped to the part its knob belongs to. Resting on a cell shows the whole frame, and a double-click opens that frame in those values. Cells are stills, which a local Chrome or Chromium takes the first time they come into view and keeps per frame, values and system revision. Until a still is ready, the cell draws the frame live. A look in the strip under the frame shows its still once one is taken. Agents can pass `sheet` to publish (`--sheet <knob>` on the CLI, `sheet` over MCP and HTTP) so the design's link opens on the grid. It is never required, and on a screen or a component it returns a nudge instead.
