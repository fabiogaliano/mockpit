The question panel is leaner. Each answer carries its own actions as icons: Comment, More like this, Archive, and ⋯ for Keep, Borrow and Move. The agent's suggestion is a mark on the answer's picture. A comment now belongs to one answer, picked or not, so changing your pick no longer moves it; agents read these comments as `onAnswers` on the answered question. More answers and Side by side are icons in the question's head, shown only when they apply. The question count sits between the arrows, and Send no longer greys out: with nothing to send, it says "Pick an answer first".
