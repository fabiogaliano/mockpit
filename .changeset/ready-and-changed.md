A screen or component now has one mark, **Ready**: a click beside its name says this is the version, and it turns blue, **Changed**, by itself when something newer shows, back to Ready when you go back to the marked version or mark it again. Marking a screen marks the components inside it too, and a dot on the mark says when one inside isn't Ready; the Tune drawer gives each its word, and Home's covers carry a small dot that opens into Ready or Changed (the word sits in the status line on a phone, where an unmarked one reads "in design"). Product designs have nothing to choose between any more: no Use this, no Which variant?, nothing put in use by a Send (`use` answers 409 with the reason); the newest version is current, and one still holding alternatives says so, with a nudge to move them to an idea. Agents read it everywhere (`read`, `read --tree`, `read --area`: `ready`, `changed`, `status`, `builtFrom`), hear your mark as `edited.ready`, and `export` hands over the Ready versions with the spec and what the design is built from (`design.json` from the CLI), which is what a build plan points at.
