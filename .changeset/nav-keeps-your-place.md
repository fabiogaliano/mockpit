The viewer keeps your place. A design reopens in the view, state and variant you left it on (Stage or Studio, per design in this browser), and its first open goes to Studio when there is a choice between variants; an agent's unanswered question still opens on Stage, and Answer next always does. The URL now names the state, variant, version and view, so a reload stays put and a copied link lands there. Back leaves for the place you came from: moving between states, questions or versions no longer adds history steps. A design's states are named tabs instead of dots, marked only when the agent is waiting on them, and Stage | Studio is a switch in the top bar. The project crumb now means the whole project and the area crumb that area, on the tab you were on.
