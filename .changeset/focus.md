A design is decided on in Focus, and there are two places now: the Board to see, Focus to decide. Enter, a double-click or Open on a Board frame opens Focus, and so does a question from Needs you; every link, copied, sent or typed, lands on the Board fit to the design or state, so Focus is never in an address. Back, Forward and a reload keep it. Focus is the design live between its states on the left (not drawn ones dashed, then its variants, or an idea's alternatives with the archived folded under "N archived") and a panel on the right with Question, Spec and Activity; Spec holds the design's Instances, and Activity is the thread. The path reads project / page / design, and Esc or the page goes back to the Board, fit to the frame and selected. On an idea the panel offers what Studio's closer look did for the alternative on stage: More like this, Borrow, Keep, Move and Archive. The Board marks the frame a question waits on with a "?". Studio (Answers, Wall, Columns, Palettes), All states and its grid, the Stage | Studio switch, the top bar's question count, Next: and Answer next with ] and [ are gone, and a design no longer opens differently by what it holds; old `?view=` and `?cols=` links land on the Board.
