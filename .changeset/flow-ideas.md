A flow can start as an idea. An idea with `becomes: flow` is a story in rough steps: its states are the steps, in order, and it opens on Stage with the flow bar named after it, so you can walk it with Try. Use this on it copies nothing: the batch's `followUps` carry `placeFlow` (the steps, each with the alternative it shows, and the screens of the idea's area), and the guide tells the agent to put each step on a screen (an existing state, a new state, or a planned screen), place the flow, set the idea's `for` to `flow:<name>`, and ask you one question on the idea to confirm, step by step. A flow step may now name a state that is declared and not drawn yet: `flow` alone on a screen's new state declares it there, `read` marks the step `planned`, and Home draws it as Not drawn. The Flows shelf says how many of each area's screen states no flow walks; a screen's cover says nothing of it.
