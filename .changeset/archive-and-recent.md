Home's Archive and Recent are easier to read. The Archive now groups what left by the day it went, with a picture on every item, what each holds, and one reason and one "Restore all" per day. Restore works on everything there, and with every design archived it brings them all back; Undo returns it to the same day. Activity is now Recent, a real history of designs updated and answers you gave, filtered to All, Decisions or Designs (`?sec=recent&show=decisions`). It's where you reword or remove a decision, replacing the Decisions row in the sidebar. When a design you put in use gets a newer version, the bar now just says so: See it, Use it, or Keep your version.
