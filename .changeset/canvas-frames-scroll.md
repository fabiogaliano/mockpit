On the canvas, each artboard now stops at the canvas's height and its page scrolls inside it. Under a finger the page takes the touch itself, so the browser scrolls it natively, with momentum, and the canvas never moves with it, not even at the page's end; drag or pinch the space around the artboards to move the canvas. A tap inside the page still selects the part under it, and Mark works as before. With a mouse, a wheel over an artboard scrolls its page.
