The Board is calmer. Screens no longer get Empty, Loading and Failed frames nobody asked for: those states are offered in the screen's header, where a click asks the agent to draw one and × says none apply. Orange now means only a question: "N questions waiting · Answer ›" at the top of the canvas opens the first one on the page, ideas included, and the counts on headers and frames are links to them. The page card is one line, with the map in a popover. A screen's frame no longer repeats the screen's name, an idea under its design is headed "Idea", and its counts say what they count. The shortcut bar shows until you press Got it, then lives behind "?". In `read --area`, Missing now means a state the user asked for.
