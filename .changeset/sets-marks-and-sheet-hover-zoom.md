Marks, Sets, Knob choice view, and Sheet hover zoom:

- **Main, Bookmark, and Delete marks**: Mark frames or sheet cells as `★ Main` (`M`), `Bookmark` (`K`) runners-up into a collapsed drawer, or `Delete` (`X`) options you don't want — deleted options vanish immediately with Undo and leave a silent `ruledOut` tombstone for the agent.
- **Pick one vs Keep all (Sets)**: Flip any idea between competing alternatives and a Set (`--set` / `--no-set` on `publish` and `edit`, MCP, and HTTP), where every live item ships together wearing its own `★ Main` per knob.
- **Knob choice panel & Side-by-side finalists**: Opening a Sheet cell opens a live stage alongside a choice panel with List and Side-by-side finalist grid modes, a collapsed Bookmarks drawer, and instant Delete with Undo.
- **Sheet in-place hover zoom & top action bar**: Replaces the detached hover preview popover on Sheet cards with an edge-locked `1.44×` in-place lift, a top action bar above the card (zero occlusion over artwork, scale-compensated action buttons), crosshair row/column highlighting, and a persistent **Hover zoom** toggle in the Sheet bar.
