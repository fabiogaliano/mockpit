Choose who gets a Send. A Send used to go to the conversation that owns the design, so when that one had gone quiet it waited unheard, even with another agent listening in the same project. While the owner has no watcher open and hasn't called in five minutes, and another session on the project is listening, a To row appears above Send: it starts on the session started in this project, and its menu lists who is listening and who isn't, by folder, last call and what else they own. What has no picker beside it (Use this, New version, a steer) goes to the same default. The session that gets the Send becomes the design's owner, hears the batch with a `handoff` note saying to read the design first, and the status names it: Delivered to onni-companion. With nobody listening nothing changes.
