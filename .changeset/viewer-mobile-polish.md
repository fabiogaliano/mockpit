Mobile polish for the viewer. A device width that fits the screen now renders at true size: over the bottom sheet the stage goes edge to edge (inside the safe area), a touch screen opens at Phone (up to 600 px wide), Tablet (up to 900) or Desktop, and the current scale ("100%", "84%") shows next to the width picker. Option pictures keep a fixed 4:3 box from the start, so nothing moves under a finger as they load. When a later answer takes a borrowed part out of Mix, Mix and the review say which answer did it. Accent-filled controls (Mark, reactions, part labels, pins) use darker fills so small white text reaches 4.5:1 in dark and light mode. Home rows no longer run off the right edge of a phone.
