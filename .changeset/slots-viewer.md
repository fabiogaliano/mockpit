The viewer understands slots. A question that chooses a slot's fill says so ("Slot · Entry"), its pictures crop to that region, and a pick outlines it on stage as "Entry · A skeleton card". A slot inside a fill gets its question right after its parent's, and only while that fill is chosen: otherwise it is skipped, marked "Only for …" if you go to it, shown struck through as "not sent" in the review, and left out of the reply. The review groups answers by state and indents nested slots. In Tune, a slot's fill is a control under its own part. Borrowing a part from another look moved from the trailing "Mix" question into Tune, as a "Borrow" line per part; the Mix question is gone. The variant view's page-embed list is now `embeds` (it was `slots`).
