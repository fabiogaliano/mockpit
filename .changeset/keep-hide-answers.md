Answers can be kept and hidden. Every answer card has a bookmark on its left to keep it for later, and on its right + for more like this one and × to hide it, which fades it away while the others slide into place; a hidden one can say why. Both save on the click and reach the agent with your next Send or More answers, and the agent never sees a hidden answer offered again: asking the same question again keeps them, and the reasons join the project's decisions. After a Send, kept answers wait beside the design and as pills on its top edge to look at without changing anything; Use this instead swaps one in without a new version, and a kept screen is no longer archived. A kept screen says how many updates it's missing, with Update it now, once, or Update it too to have the agent change it along with the one in use. Saved sets name a combination of answers across questions, to preview and put in use whole. Studio gains Answers: the open question laid out at once, culled with ←→, K, X, Enter and Z, and On every state to see each answer across the states.
