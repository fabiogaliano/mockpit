A screen or component has a way back after any new version or use, marked Ready or not: a Back pill to the version shown before (the Ready one when marked), with no countdown. Back is a stay, so the design shows that version until something newer comes, which takes the stage again; on an unmarked design × keeps the newest and puts the pill away. `stay` no longer answers 409 on a design with nothing marked Ready, only on an entry with no earlier version.
