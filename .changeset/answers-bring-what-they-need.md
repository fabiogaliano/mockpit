More answers no longer means a new version. An answer can bring what it needs to draw itself (`style` or `fill` beside a `set` of a new value, or `html` for a whole page), so its picture is right at once and the version in use stays as it is. A whole page becomes a version only if it's chosen, and Send puts it in use with no further step. While you choose, neighbouring pictures stay on the page in use, a picture of one part shows that part, a card landing under a still mouse doesn't start a preview, and New version steps aside for Send.
