Answers reach the design on stage reliably. The stage now opens with your answers already applied (they ride in the frame's address, as option pictures already did), and every pick or preview reaches it as soon as the page starts, even while it is still loading a slow stylesheet such as a web font; before, such a page showed its bare markup and ignored taps until it finished loading. When a pick changes the design, the part it changed is outlined on stage for a moment, and brought into view if it was out of sight.
