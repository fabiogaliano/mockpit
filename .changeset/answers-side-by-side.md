A question beside the design has One | Side by side. Side by side lays the design as it stands (Current) and every answer at full height in a row on the canvas, in place of the design's frame and its states; the row scales to fit, then scrolls sideways. Clicking an answer there picks it, outlined and named Chosen while the rest dim; clicking it again takes the pick back. Answers that depend on the state are drawn on the state on stage. Questions answered in words grey the toggle out and say why. The layout is remembered per design in the browser. After a Send the question stays beside the design as answered, and its row stays on the canvas: the pick outlined, kept answers bookmarked beside it, archived ones folded under "N archived". This replaces the kept-answer pills on the design's top edge for questions with pictures, and a Send made side by side no longer moves on to the next design by itself.
