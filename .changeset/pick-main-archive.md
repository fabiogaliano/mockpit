Pick, Main and Archive on any frame of an idea. Hovering a frame, or a sheet cell, shows a pill: Pick, Main (a star, one per state, which can be moved), Archive, More like this and Comment. P, M and X do the same from the keyboard. Picks and Main are saved on the click, stored with the design and sent to no one. Focus's list of alternatives and the sheet show a tick or a star, and an archived frame leaves for the Archive with Undo in the toast. A sheet cell is marked on its own: the frame in that value or look. Answering a question whose answers are alternatives picks that frame too, the same record the tick writes. Pick replaces Keep on an answer's row, and a kept answer reads as a pick. A Send never archives a picked alternative, as it never archived a kept one. `read` (CLI, MCP and HTTP) adds `marks`: each pick, Main and archived frame with its state, alternative, look or values, when and by whom, kept answers included with the answer they come from. A reply's legacy `tuned` field now carries the Main frame's values, falling back to the alternative in use, then the one on screen. A screen or a component gets no pill: nothing on it is picked, and Ready stays its mark.
